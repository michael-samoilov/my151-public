Custom Pokédex Maker

my151's custom Pokédex builder lets you create a custom region by filling a "paint-by-numbers" template of archetypes like starters, regional bird, Pikaclone, pseudo-legendary, and more. Hand-pick your ideal region of 151 Pokémon from all 1,025, then compare the type distribution of your picks with the National Dex, and export a PNG of your dex. Made for fans planning a dream region and fan-game developers planning a regional dex. Free, in the browser, no sign-up.
