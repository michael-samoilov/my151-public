The slot the two would share
What the geometry has to fit before any of the four splits is worth arguing about.
GYM LEADER TEAM
148 x 204 · 3 columns x 4 rows
The slot the two would share is the card's top-left void: three columns wide and four rows tall, which is portrait by a third. That shape is what separates the four geometries — halving it across the short side leaves two landscape faces, halving it along the long side leaves two narrow ones.

The partner is not restricted to the team. Where the signature picker locks to the card's own lines once the template is full, the partner picker stays open to all 1,025 — a partner is the leader's companion, not their sixth Pokémon.

It is still seated when there is room: choosing one while an evolution line is free places it there, so a partner on a five-line card becomes the sixth line AND the partner. On a full card it lives in the slot alone.
Four geometries follow, each in all four states: empty, signature only, partner only, both.
