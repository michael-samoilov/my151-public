What shipped

  • Where the slot number goes. The card's bar has no room for "Gym 1" on its own once the identity block takes the left, so the mock folds it into the heading: "Pro Trainer Card". The alternative is dropping it, since the card's own top band already says which slot it is.
  • The toggle's labels — settled as "Story" and "Rematch", in the dex builder's own filled/ghost button pair without its icons.
  • The type chip beside the dashed line — settled. The underline runs under the theme only, and the chip sits beside it, centred on the same axis as the words.
  • Losing the information icon. Nothing else on the league home explains what a spacer row is; the (i) was the only door to it.
